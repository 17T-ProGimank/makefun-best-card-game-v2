Tidepool Tussle — a duel of the tidepools. You are the Guardian of the Pools, duelling Pell, King of the Tidepools, with a deck of 24 sea creatures across four families: Wave, Shell, Coral and Kelp. Spend bubbles to summon creatures, attack Pell's creatures or Pell himself, and drain his Tide from 20 to 0 before he drains yours. Round 2 adds 8 new creatures and a hero power for each side: your Tide Surge (once per game: +3 bubbles, +3 Tide) against Pell's Royal Blessing, which fires on its own the first time his Tide drops to 10 and restores 4. A complete 1v1 card battler that plays offline in a desktop browser: title screen with rules, a full game loop with an AI opponent, and win and loss screens.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```
